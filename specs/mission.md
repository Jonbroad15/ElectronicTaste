# Mission

## Electronic Taste

**Electronic Taste** is a mobile application designed for ravers, electronic music enthusiasts, and anyone curious about the vast world of electronic music subgenres.

### What We're Building

An app that listens to the music around you — at a club, a festival, or through your headphones — and instantly identifies the electronic music subgenre you're hearing. Think of it as a "Shazam for electronic subgenres."

### How It Works

1. **Listen** — The user opens the app and the microphone captures the audio playing around them.
2. **Analyze** — The app extracts key audio features (BPM, instrumentation, vocal presence, rhythmic patterns, etc.) and feeds them into a machine learning model.
3. **Predict** — The model classifies the audio into an electronic music subgenre (e.g., techno, house, drum & bass, trance, dubstep, hardstyle, etc.).
4. **Rate** — Users provide feedback: Do they like this track? How much? This builds a personal taste profile over time.
5. **Recommend** — Over time, the app learns what the user enjoys and recommends new artists and styles they're likely to love.

### Why It Matters

Electronic music has hundreds of subgenres, and navigating that landscape can be overwhelming — even for seasoned ravers. Electronic Taste bridges that gap by helping users discover *what* they're hearing, track their preferences, and find more of what they love.

### Core Principles

- **Discovery First** — The app should make exploring electronic music feel effortless and fun.
- **Learn from the User** — Every rating and interaction makes the recommendations smarter.
- **Real-World Use** — Built for loud clubs and festival stages, not just quiet rooms.
- **Community-Driven Data** — As users categorize and rate music, the collective data strengthens the model for everyone.
